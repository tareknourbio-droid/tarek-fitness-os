How to experiment

1. Drag a molecule by touching one of its atoms.
2. Rotate the whole 3D view by dragging empty space; pinch to zoom.
3. Bring a partially positive H (δ+) near another molecule's O (δ−).
4. Watch the dashed line when a suitable hydrogen-bond arrangement is detected.
